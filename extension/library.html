How reviews work

FSRS schedules each card from your ratings, using a 90% target retention and default parameters. Again means forgotten; Hard means recalled with effort; Good means recalled; Easy means effortless. Suspending a card pauses it. Undo reverses the latest rating across your tabs.

Using Malnaru Read

Enable a regular webpage using the toolbar. Hover Korean text, select up to eight Korean words, or press Alt+Shift+K for lookup. Shortcut settings are at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Disable on this page stops lookup and highlighting until enabled again. Embedded articles need Chrome website access to their origin. Browser/store pages, PDFs and image lettering are unsupported. Dynamic scans are bounded to 10,000 text nodes and 500,000 characters.
