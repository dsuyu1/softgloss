Dictionary, grammar and scheduler credits

Offline spaced repetition uses unmodified ts-fsrs 5.4.2 by Open Spaced Repetition under the MIT license. Upstream source. Malnaru Read uses default FSRS parameters, a 90% target retention and 1/10 minute learning steps. No personal parameter training or Anki synchronization is provided.

Bilingual dictionary sentence examples adapt original usage examples in the same pinned English Wiktionary/Kaikki source under CC BY-SA 4.0. Examples remain linked to their source sense IDs and entry history URLs. Selection: source type example, no reference/quotation field, both Korean and English, Korean terminal sentence punctuation, length bounds and at most two pairs per sense. Quotations and fragments are excluded. These are source examples, not independently verified translations. No audio is imported.

Default study vocabulary and grammar adapt the National Institute of Korean Language (국립국어원), 2017 International Standard Curriculum for Korean Language Education, vocabulary/grammar grade appendix revised 2020-11-17. Author: Kim Jung-sup (김중섭) and research team; publisher: NIKL. Source: official report and downloadable appendix. Reused under Korea Open Government License (공공누리) Type 1, attribution required. Changes: retain grade and form fields, normalize homonym digits/alternate headwords, collapse lemma duplicates to the lowest source grade, omit vocabulary examples, generate offline scanning data. These are curriculum grades, not an exhaustive TOPIK exam syllabus; no endorsement is implied. All 336 grammar records are included, but recognition covers supported morphology and expression patterns, not every possible inflection.

The three particle teaching notes in grammar.js adapt Wiktionary particle references under CC BY-SA 4.0; each note links to its reference and contributor history. Explanations are simplified and examples are newly written for Malnaru Read. Independent Korean-language review remains pending.

Pronunciation audio

Packaged pronunciation clips for curriculum words are machine-generated with Supertonic 3 by Supertone Inc., preset voice F1. They are synthetic speech, not recordings of a person. The model is licensed under the BigScience OpenRAIL-M License. It is used only to generate the clips; no model weights are distributed with Malnaru Read. Supertone claims no rights in generated output, and the license's use restrictions, including disclosure of machine-generated content and no impersonation, apply to these clips. Words without a clip may use a voice installed on your device. Malnaru Read only selects voices Chrome explicitly marks as non-remote.
