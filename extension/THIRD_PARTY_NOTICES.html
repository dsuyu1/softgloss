SoftGloss changes: select supported parts of speech with modern Hangul headwords; retain definitions, sense IDs, labels, hanja spellings and selected indicative conjugations; sort, deduplicate and compact the data. Examples, quotations, audio, images, etymologies and cross-reference-only definitions are omitted. This is a dictionary extract, with possible source or extraction errors.

The initial original fallback glosses remain for entries absent from this pack. No third-party examples, media or frequency corpus is bundled. Listed forms do not establish the intended meaning in a sentence. Dialect, register and usage labels reflect the upstream data.

The supplemental conjugation pack adapts additional table rows from the same pinned source. Forms are compacted as Hangul transformations restricted to their original entry IDs. The additional morphology snapshot, hashes and importer are included in the repository. Possible compound and derived-predicate results show each component’s source; they do not supply an inferred whole-word definition.

Rebuild instructions, the source lock and checksums are in the source repository. The upstream download changes over time; rebuild only from a snapshot matching the recorded hash.
